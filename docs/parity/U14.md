# Parity — U14 (survival interactions)

U14 delivers `bot.fish()` (`lib/plugins/fishing.js`), `bot.writeBook` / `bot.signBook` (`lib/plugins/book.js`),
`bot.sleep` / `bot.wake` (`lib/plugins/bed.js`, whose state part is U9's) and the e2e ports of the upstream `fishing`,
`book`, `bed` and `elytra` tests, plus the Bedrock extra `riding` (boat / horse control with `moveVehicle`, U5, after
`mount`, U7). Everything was observed against BDS 1.26.51.1 (protocol 2193); `adapted` rows link
[docs/differences/U14.md](../differences/U14.md).

Unit tests replay the recorded join on a fake client with the packet shapes recorded live
(`test/unit/survival.test.js`, the U14 half of `test/unit/bed.test.js`).

Bedrock extras without a reference row:
- `packages/mineflayer-bedrock/test/external/riding.test.js` › "boat on open water: forward, turn, stop, dismount next
  to it" and "tamed, saddled horse: forward, stop, dismount next to it" — riding control, no correction storm (see
  [differences: riding](../differences/U14.md#riding)).
- `packages/mineflayer-bedrock/test/external/fishing.test.js` › "the bobber disappears → 'Fishing cancelled'; fish()
  again → the first call rejects (Bedrock extra)", `book.test.js` › "held book: write, rewrite with fewer pages
  (delete_page), sign (Bedrock extra)", `bed.test.js` › "survival: the server keeps the player in the bed, marks it
  occupied, wakes it after 100 ticks (Bedrock extra)" and "sleep() rejections like upstream: by day, not a bed, too far
  (Bedrock extra)".

Pending on wave-3 fix-ups (run-time `this.skip()` with the reason, the assertions start running once they are on
main):
- `elytra` firework boost (`usedFirework`, `fireworkRocketDuration` counting down): W3-FIX-B (firework tracking reads
  the boosted player from the rocket's metadata key 18) and W3-FIX-A (a correction during a boost erases it).
- `riding` boat: the rider getting off at its mount position (physics reports the mount position while riding).
