# Parity — U7 (entities & players)

U7 owns every entity other than the bot and every player (`lib/plugins/entities.js`, taking over from U4, whose
local-player part is kept), `lib/bedrock/paintings.js`, the prismarine-entity patch (`getDroppedItem` /
`getCustomName` on Bedrock) and the `bot.test` entity helpers. Packet handling is unit-tested on a fake client with the
recorded BDS join plus synthesized packets (`test/unit/entities.test.js`); behaviour is asserted against a real BDS
1.26.51.1 by `test/external/{displayName,entities,players,vehicles,animals,mobFights}.test.js` (the last five are
Bedrock extras). `adapted` / `n.a.` rows link [docs/differences/U7.md](../differences/U7.md).

Gate: deterministic suites (displayName, entities, players, vehicles) 3/3; random suites (animals — retries bound
the randomness; mobFights — AI, knockback, creeper fuse) ≥ 4/5. Results are in the PR.

Pending on siblings (written, `this.skip()` until the feature lands):
- `usedFirework` / `bot.fireworkRocketDuration` while really flying: the elytra test is wave 4 (U14); unit-tested here.
