# Parity — U11 (placing & placement-based parity)

U11 delivers block and entity placement (`lib/plugins/generic_place.js`, `place_block.js` vendored verbatim,
`place_entity.js`, the shared Bedrock internals in `lib/bedrock/placement.js`), the real `bot.test.placeBlock`
(`test/external/common/helpers/place.js`) and the placement-based upstream tests (placeBlock, placeEntity, useChests
verbatim, sign, nether, the place half of digAndBuild) plus Bedrock extras: sneak-placing against interactive blocks,
buckets, flint and steel, bone meal, projectiles (itemUse), a self-built nether portal (portalIgnition) and the
pathfinder-critical pillar / bridge scenarios (scaffold). Unit tests: `test/unit/placement.test.js`. `adapted` / `n.a.`
rows link [docs/differences/U11.md](../differences/U11.md).
